Provisional south-wall kitchen exhaust

Conceptual coordination only. NOT an installation design or permission to cut. The hood/appliance, terminal product, structural drawings and actual site conditions have not been selected or verified.

The original sketch marks SOUTH on drawing-left, NORTH on drawing-right, WEST at the rear and EAST at the entrance. The retained suspended hood now discharges through a high solid south-wall pier beside the kitchen window, not through the roof. Four slender illustrative ceiling-suspension rods are separate from the round exhaust pipe; their fixings and load path remain unengineered.

Furnished Cutaway omits the upper duct, suspension rods and outside termination as a component-based presentation choice. The capture canopy, underside details, fridge and cabinets remain visible. Exterior and Interior restore the complete service assembly. This is not a changed route or a physical cut to the CAD/IFC model.

Manufacturer reference actually read

Bosch official installation manual 9001096092_F.pdf, English printed pages 4, 7 and 14: metal ducting and outdoor discharge; short, smooth, adequately sized routing; that manual's recommended nominal 150 mm round duct; slight outward fall; an exterior wall sleeve and back-pressure flap. This is a different appliance and jurisdiction, used for general visual coordination only. It does not approve the generic 800 x 600 mm suspended hood, the illustrated terminal or Indian installation clearances.

The referenced Broan NTM and rangehood PDFs could not be retrieved (HTTP 403); their search extracts were not treated as inspected installation instructions.

What still needs professional coordination

The proposal clears the represented window/glazing/frame and EXAMPLE beams/columns geometrically. That overlay is not a real structural design. In particular, actual window lintels and their bearings, hidden reinforcement/services, masonry stability and opening permission are unknown. The terminal remains near an operable window; exhaust-to-window/boundary separation, re-entry and nuisance, airflow/pressure loss, make-up air, fire/grease provisions, cleaning access, power, support, wall sleeve, corrosion protection, flashing and weather sealing all need a qualified local designer/installer and the chosen appliance/terminal manuals. No beam, column, window or roof is cut by the proposal.
