Timber home mandir

An original domestic mandir for the existing Prayer room, using warm timber, a cusped torana, turned front pillars, open diamond side jaali, a pale backdrop with floral brass relief, a stone altar and two storage drawers. Three hollow bells with clappers and suspension rods, a supported marigold toran, a kalash with coconut/mango leaves, two unlit samai lamps and an offering thali replace the blank panel and two cylinders.

No deity, sect, sacred inscription or user-specific religious preference is assumed. The floral centre and offering arrangement leave the choice of a preferred image or murti to the household. This is illustrative furniture, not a copied catalogue product, carpenter's drawing or verified installation/fire-safety design. Lamps are unlit; no flame, incense or heat-source installation is specified.
