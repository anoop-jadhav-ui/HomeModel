Furniture scale and free-walking review

Measured from actual transformed meshes, in millimetres. Each size is world X x world Y x height, followed by its top above finished floor. Rotated furniture swaps plan axes. The1625.6mm person and1524mm eye datum are unchanged.

Generic furnishings, not a purchasing schedule or installation/ergonomic certification. CAD/IFC, room dimensions, door openings and the other29 furniture footprints/locations remain unchanged. The Prayer mandir alone has a revised depth/height at its existing centre/width; see references and scale. The tall488mm toilet seats, compact792mm shower trays and compact eight-chair dining arrangement need actual product/user trials before depending on comfort or usable clearances.

Outcome

No feet/metres conversion or wholesale furniture overscale defect was found. The dining seats were actually491mm, not the previously cited460mm reference. They are now460mm, with a matching750mm-high table instead of783mm. Supports and tabletop props follow the revised heights. Other measured dimensions are retained rather than arbitrarily shrinking items to make circulation easier.

    Walking correction

    Free W/A/S/D and touch movement no longer automatically turn toward a doorway or cardinal room axis. Cancelling deliberate room navigation also cancels its pending turn without discarding momentum or later user mouse look. Next/Previous/room selection remain deliberate guided navigation. The old50ms frame cap discarded elapsed movement time below20fps (one held-forward second moved1.265ft at10fps versus3.000ft at60fps). The viewer/controller now preserve intervals through100ms, still bounding longer stalls and substepping collision movement at no more than0.15ft. Walls, furniture, frames, closed doors, moving-door sweeps and the existing1.1ft-diameter collision envelope are not bypassed or enlarged.

    Floor/step eye height remains exact; there is no vertical camera lag or head bob. This removes reproduced steering/timing defects, not a guarantee of render frame rate on a physical phone. Native architectural files and parked services work are untouched. Re-run node furniture-scale-audit.mjs after changing furnishings to refresh this generated audit.
