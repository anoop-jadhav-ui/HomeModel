Conceptual structure study

NOT ENGINEERED. NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION. This separate viewer mode is a discussion diagram, not a structural scheme, sizing exercise, analysis model or approval. No structural drawings, soil report, loading, site survey, wind/seismic parameters, jurisdiction or design-code inputs have been supplied.

Reading the diagram

Roof envelope toggleExisting architectural hip-roof and monitor-cap outlines, not rafters, purlins, trusses or a designed roof diaphragm.

Editable source and shared geometry

output\structure-settings.js centralizes architectural wall-axis references, selected station indices, colors and column-marker display width. The 0.30-ft marker width is a graphic symbol, not a real member section. It must not be used to order materials or size columns. Beam axes intentionally have no physical section dimensions.

output\structural-concept.js derives marker locations, floor/wall-top/monitor levels, slab and roof/wall reference geometry from the same review-data.js used by all other modes. In the current model, local grade is +2 ft, FFL +4 ft and wall top +14 ft; this is not a surveyed/sea-level datum. Updates must regenerate the architectural export first, then the diagram consumes those updated references. Missing references fail explicitly.

All diagram objects live in a separate hidden-by-default group with provisional/non-engineered metadata. Exterior, Cutaway, Walk/Fly and Plan retain their approved architectural/furnishing geometry. Mode changes alter visibility and camera only. Structure mode hides the actual furnished scene and offers independent context/envelope toggles; leaving it restores the prior furniture/roof preferences.

Native CAD / IFC and review boundary

Home.FCStd, Home.ifc, the parametric architectural inputs, source sketches and archives are retained unchanged. This branch does not add guessed structural objects to those architectural files and does not provide a structural IFC/CAD package. The editable conceptual source is the separate viewer module/settings. No structural quantities, loads, foundation sizes, reinforcement, safety factors or construction details are generated.

A licensed local structural engineer must determine the actual gravity/lateral system, column/wall/beam layout, bearing, member sizes, joints, slab system, foundations, soil response and roof/monitor transfer paths. In particular, the open central space and raised monitor must be resolved rather than assuming a rectangle drawn in a viewer is supported. Construction, demolition or procurement must not be based on this study.
