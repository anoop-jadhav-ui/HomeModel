Jadhav Nivaas: furniture realism study

This study uses public product descriptions and dimensions as references for generic construction, proportions and surface behavior. It is not a reproduction, endorsement, purchasing schedule or measured manufacturer model. The earlier furnishing-only pass preserved architectural geometry; the current user-directed revision changes only the main door's single leaf into a native paired assembly in the same opening. Other architectural geometry, room layout and site remain unchanged.

References and what they informed

  • IKEA DYTÅG textiles: irregular linen texture and a relaxed, rather than rigid, fabric silhouette. In the model: authored fine weave, shaped cover folds, side/foot drape and pillow welting.
  • Muuto Outline Soft: layered foam/wadding, loose fabric folds and distinct cushion/frame construction. Its listed 45-cm seat height informed the modeled sofa's approximately 45-cm seat surface; the sofa's existing footprint is retained. No proprietary outline or upholstery pattern was copied.
  • IKEA VIMLE: separate back/seat/arm assemblies and the relation of seat depth, arm height and under-sofa clearance. The official indexed dimensions were available; direct page access returned 403. The model is not a VIMLE replica.
  • TON No. 14: a lightweight timber chair construction and 46-cm seat-height reference. The original modeled chairs now have a generic open lower back, curved upper back rail, splayed supports and stretchers, not the No. 14's protected design. The page required JavaScript; dimensions were available in its indexed manufacturer description.
  • IKEA AXSTAD fronts: inset panel depth, restrained matte finish and visible frame/reveal relationships. The model uses independently authored stepped panel geometry, mounted pulls and recessed toe kicks, not downloaded cabinet fronts.
  • Duravit D-Neo washbowl: rounded ceramic lips, shallow inner profiles and a central waste position. Its indexed 600 x 400 x 125-mm example informed the profile relationship, not fixture sizing; existing fixture envelopes/locations are retained. Direct access returned 403.
  • Hindware Flamingo pressed kitchen sink: conventional Indian-market single-bowl stainless construction, rounded internal corners, matte finish and practical inset rim. Its page lists 610 x 460 x 203 mm. This informs the kitchen only, not the bathroom basins.
  • Anupam 205B: inset rim, deep utensil-washing bowl and central waste. The reference has a drainboard; the model retains its existing counter arrangement rather than copying the product or adding an unrequested drainboard. The generic authored sink is approximately 701 x 494 mm overall, with a 604 x 390 mm opening and approximately 189 mm depth. Dimensions are illustrative, not a purchase/installation schedule.

Runtime assets and licenses

All furniture meshes, cloth/wood/jute/stone surface data, door hardware and the clothed scale figure are authored procedurally in furnishings.js, furniture-surfaces.js, door-hardware.js, walker-avatar.js and kitchen-fixtures.js. No reference photograph, commercial model, downloaded texture or copied product design is included in runtime assets.

Three.js MIT license covers the locally vendored Three.js 0.180.0 library, unmodified RoundedBoxGeometry, RoomEnvironment and Reflector. RoomEnvironment supplies generic studio-style material illumination, generated once on the GPU, for furniture and decorative door hardware. It is not a literal reflection of the home. Designated bathroom mirrors instead use actual view-dependent planar scene reflection. No online HDR/environment asset is loaded. Existing font licenses remain in their original notices.

Review and limits

The pass includes suspended timber bed rails, channelled upholstery, a closed two-skin draped duvet, smooth tailored cushions, seat piping, actual inset cabinet fronts and mounted hardware, open chair backs/supports, rolled ceramic rims and smoother seat rings. Cloth, painted fronts, timber, stone, glazed ceramic and metal have distinct surface responses.

Matched 1600 x 1000 comparisons and exact camera poses are saved under review\realism-v2 for bedroom, living, dining, kitchen and bathroom. The development review is isolated from the approved preview; it is not a production deployment.

The furniture remains within 360 meshes and 60,000 triangles. Seven shared 128 x 128 authored textures contain wood, cloth and stone albedo/relief pairs plus the retained jute map: 458,752 raw source bytes before mipmaps. Repeated cabinet pulls are batched per asset; their separate collision bounds are preserved, so batching does not create invisible obstacles across the kitchen. The figure, architectural door hardware and planar mirror passes are additional scene resources, reported separately in review evidence. Browser viewport checks are not certification of performance on a physical phone. These are lightweight authored assets, not scanned or ray-traced photorealism. Door paths and room fit are checked, but fixture-use, structural, accessibility and building-code clearances remain unverified.

Interaction and door presentation

Tap/click an unobstructed bedside lamp as a hidden interaction; there is no dashboard lamp panel or upfront discovery prompt. With the scene focused, L provides a non-prominent accessible equivalent for the nearest actually visible lamp, with a non-visual state announcement. No hidden switch buttons remain focusable. Each lamp keeps its own state across modes and furniture visibility. A warm shade/bulb response and finite-range, non-shadowing local point light are used. Walking does not extinguish switched lamps. Drag, pinch, hold, UI interaction, active movement and occluded hits do not switch hidden lamps. Plan/hidden furniture disable lights without erasing their state. There is no night-time lighting system; unshadowed local lights can spill across nearby geometry.

Hardware, kitchen chimney and paired-entry revision

The hardware audit found existing pulls and door handle blocks, but missing upper-cabinet, media-console and prayer-cabinet hardware. It also found pulls floating in front of recessed panels. All 44 cabinet/drawer pulls now mount on actual inset faces, with joined posts and mounting plates. Door blocks are replaced by leaf-mounted approximately104-mm levers,50-mm roses, key escutcheons and three illustrated hinges per leaf. Hardware follows each hinge during animation; it is not duplicated as static geometry. Fronts, wood grain, fabric weave and granular stone use independently authored surfaces with metric UVs, not one stretched texture per item. Kitchen worktops now read as restrained dark granite; architectural floors are unchanged.

Single scale walker and actual mirrors

The neutral clothed figure is authored locally at exactly1625.6mm (5ft4in) crown stature. Eyes are1524mm (60in), with an illustrative101.6-mm crown-to-eye offset. The JSON stature/offset drive both avatar and all grounded camera poses above the current grade, each tread or FFL. Camera and feet use the same local datum without a vertical camera lag. Only one person exists: it moves with grounded Walk, remains at the last floor-supported pose during Fly, and appears there in Exterior/Cutaway. The primary first-person camera omits head/upper-body layers to avoid selfie clipping; legs/shoes remain visible when looking down. This is a simplified standing-pose moving scale figure, not motion capture or a gait simulation. Collision clearance remains an approximate existing avatar envelope, not anthropometric or accessibility certification.

All three vanity mirrors have real planar reflections of visible architecture, furniture, switched lamp state and the complete avatar layer, not a stock HDR/photo. Mounting was raised to put this person's face within the small mirror aperture; their retained size naturally shows face/upper body rather than inventing a full-length reflection. Mirror normals face the room; the virtual camera clips geometry behind the glass. Only the best visible, unoccluded mirror updates per frame, in Interior Walk/Fly:512 x512 desktop or256 x256 at initial mobile viewport, no multisampling or recursive mirror bounce. Other/inactive mirrors show explicitly matte gray-green backing, not a false frozen photograph. Exterior/Cutaway use this matte fallback. Whole-frame render counters include the mirror pass. These are rasterized one-bounce reflections, not ray-traced glass/refraction or perfect multiple-mirror transport.

Fly's primary camera excludes the complete person even when flying through its last grounded pose. Walk keeps lower-body visibility without its own head; Exterior/Cutaway and the independent reflection camera retain the full same person. Upper and lower body have separate layers from building geometry. Camera policy is applied on mode changes and every frame, without moving the figure, increasing near clip or temporarily hiding the person globally during a reflection. Before/after overlap renders and actual main/reflection render-list checks are saved with the review evidence.
