Jadhav Nivaas: furniture realism study

This second pass uses public product descriptions and dimensions as references for generic construction, proportions and surface behavior. It is not a reproduction, endorsement, purchasing schedule or measured manufacturer model. The architectural FreeCAD/IFC files, room layout and site remain unchanged.

References and what they informed

  • IKEA DYTÅG textiles: irregular linen texture and a relaxed, rather than rigid, fabric silhouette. In the model: authored fine weave, shaped cover folds, side/foot drape and pillow welting.
  • Muuto Outline Soft: layered foam/wadding, loose fabric folds and distinct cushion/frame construction. Its listed 45-cm seat height informed the modeled sofa's approximately 45-cm seat surface; the sofa's existing footprint is retained. No proprietary outline or upholstery pattern was copied.
  • IKEA VIMLE: separate back/seat/arm assemblies and the relation of seat depth, arm height and under-sofa clearance. The official indexed dimensions were available; direct page access returned 403. The model is not a VIMLE replica.
  • TON No. 14: a lightweight timber chair construction and 46-cm seat-height reference. The original modeled chairs now have a generic open lower back, curved upper back rail, splayed supports and stretchers, not the No. 14's protected design. The page required JavaScript; dimensions were available in its indexed manufacturer description.
  • IKEA AXSTAD fronts: inset panel depth, restrained matte finish and visible frame/reveal relationships. The model uses independently authored stepped panel geometry, mounted pulls and recessed toe kicks, not downloaded cabinet fronts.
  • Duravit D-Neo washbowl: rounded ceramic lips, shallow inner profiles and a central waste position. Its indexed 600 x 400 x 125-mm example informed the profile relationship, not fixture sizing; existing fixture envelopes/locations are retained. Direct access returned 403.

References checked 25 November 2026. Pinterest was not needed. Product descriptions and indexed dimensions are inspiration, not verified installation specifications.

Runtime assets and licenses

All furniture meshes, cloth/wood surface data and decorative details are authored procedurally in furnishings.js. No reference photograph, commercial model, downloaded texture or copied product design is included in runtime assets.

Three.js MIT license covers the locally vendored Three.js 0.180.0 library, unmodified RoundedBoxGeometry and unmodified RoomEnvironment. The latter supplies an authored studio-style reflection environment, generated once on the GPU. It applies only to furniture materials, not architectural walls, base, doors or landscape. No online HDR/environment asset is loaded. Existing font licenses remain in their original notices.

Review and limits

The pass includes suspended timber bed rails, channelled upholstery, a closed two-skin draped duvet, smooth tailored cushions, seat piping, actual inset cabinet fronts and mounted hardware, open chair backs/supports, rolled ceramic rims and smoother seat rings. Cloth, painted fronts, timber, stone, glazed ceramic and metal have distinct surface responses.

Matched 1600 x 1000 comparisons and exact camera poses are saved under review\realism-v2 for bedroom, living, dining, kitchen and bathroom. The development review is isolated from the approved preview; it is not a production deployment.

The existing limits remain 360 furniture meshes and 60,000 triangles, with shared buffers and two 128 x 128 authored surface textures. Browser viewport checks are not certification of performance on a physical phone. These are improved lightweight authored assets, not scanned or ray-traced photorealism. Reflections are a generic image-based environment, not a live reflection of the home. Door paths and room fit are checked, but fixture-use, structural, accessibility and building-code clearances remain unverified.
